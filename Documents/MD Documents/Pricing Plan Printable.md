## How Transfer Payments Work

Old way → Cashier tells customer: *"Send to this account"*  
Problem → Employee could give their own account number

**Quik Shopping way → The app generates a one-time temporary account for every transfer transaction.** The customer sends to that account. The payment provider confirms it automatically. No employee ever touches the routing.

This eliminates one of the most common forms of internal retail fraud in Nigeria.
