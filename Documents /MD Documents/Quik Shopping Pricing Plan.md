# Quik Shopping Pricing Strategy - Business Plan

**Date:** August 2025  
**Product:** Quik Shopping - Professional Point of Sale System  
**Target Market:** Nigerian businesses (with global accessibility)

---

## Market Positioning

**Competitive Advantage:**
- 75-85% less expensive than competitors (₦200-300k/month)
- True mobility - works on mobile and desktop
- Global accessibility - works from anywhere
- Modern, user-friendly interface
- Cloud-based with real-time sync

---

## Pricing Tiers

### Professional Plan (Larger Stores)
**Monthly:** ₦50,000/month  
**Annual:** ₦540,000/year (10% discount - saves ₦60,000)

**Features:**
- Unlimited products
- Unlimited transaction history
- Multiple admin accounts
- Advanced reporting & analytics
- Priority customer support
- Multi-location support
- Custom receipt templates
- Inventory management
- Sales analytics dashboard

---

### Starter Plan (Smaller Stores)
**Monthly:** ₦12,000/month  
**Annual:** ₦129,600/year (10% discount - saves ₦14,400)

**Features:**
- Up to 500 products
- 6 months transaction history
- Single admin account
- Basic reporting
- Standard customer support
- Single location
- Standard receipt templates
- Basic inventory tracking

---

## Value Proposition

**For Large Stores:**
