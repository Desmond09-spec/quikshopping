## 1. Advanced Inventory Management System

### 1.1 Comprehensive Stock Management
**Priority**: High | **Timeline**: Q1 2026

#### Features to Implement:
- **Automated Stock Tracking**: Real-time inventory adjustments based on sales
- **Reorder Point Management**: Automatic low-stock alerts and reorder suggestions
- **Supplier Integration**: Vendor management with automated purchase order generation
- **Stock Valuation Methods**: Support for FIFO, LIFO, and weighted average costing
- **Multi-location Inventory**: Track stock across multiple store locations

#### Business Benefits:
- Reduces manual inventory management by 75%
- Prevents stockouts and overstock situations
- Improves cash flow through optimized purchasing
- Provides accurate financial reporting for inventory valuation

#### Technical Requirements:
- Enhanced database schema for supplier and stock movement tracking
- Automated alert system with email/SMS notifications
- Integration APIs for supplier communication
- Advanced reporting engine for inventory analytics
